    Academic Research Project

    Hybrid Deep Learning Model for Fake News Detection Using BERT Multimodal Features, Explainable AI and Firebase

    Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Vels Institute of Science, Technology & Advanced Studies (VISTAS)

    Abstract

    Digital misinformation poses critical challenges to information integrity. This system implements a hybrid deep learning architecture combining 64-dimensional BERT transformer contextual embeddings with stylometric, source credibility, and network diffusion features (79 dimensions total). Inferences are coupled with dual Explainable AI (SHAP attributions and token-level self-attention heatmaps) and continuous concept drift telemetry.
